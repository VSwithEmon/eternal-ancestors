Meet Merilyn

A proud Trawlwoolway woman, change-maker and co-designer.

Merilyn Duff founded Eternal Ancestors in 2023 after more than fifteen years working across Aboriginal health, community services and government in Victoria. Her work is about creating new ways of thinking, and breaking down stigma and discrimination.

She has led Aboriginal health and wellbeing programs at EACH, managed integrated Aboriginal services at Eastern Health, served as a Senior Cultural Advisor with the Victorian Department of Families, Fairness and Housing, and worked as Health Coordinator at Mullum Mullum Indigenous Gathering Place.

Healing

Specialised cultural support after violence

A healing journey program for Aboriginal and/or Torres Strait Islander people who have experienced violence. It can include a smoking ceremony and clearing of the home, and the making of a new possum skin cloak to begin recording a new story, solo or as a family.
